Methods

Version 3 data and health model

Annual PM2.5 fields for 2020–2023 are regridded to 0.25° × 0.25° after treating −999 as missing. GEMM estimates all-cause PM2.5-attributable mortality among adults aged 25+ using each year’s GBD NCD+LRI baseline mortality. GPW 2020 population and SSP1 2015 age structure are held constant, so year-to-year differences reflect changes in PM2.5 and baseline mortality rather than complete demographic trends. Country concentrations use exact World Bank polygon-to-grid area fractions; country population and gridded mortality use a GPW 2020 30 arc-second population-weighted mask. World Bank non-determined legal status areas remain unassigned and are shown separately. City-window concentrations are population-weighted. Avoidable burden compares current exposure with the WHO guideline of 5 μg/m3. Displayed 90% uncertainty intervals are the 5th–95th percentiles from GEMM parameters and do not include uncertainty in PM2.5, population, baseline mortality, or spatial representation.

City-centered method

Nine grid cells, not a city boundary

Each city estimate uses the grid cell nearest its coordinate plus the eight surrounding cells. At 35° latitude this 3 × 3 window covers approximately 75 x 75 km, or 5,700 km²; the selected city’s actual latitude-adjusted window area is shown in the panel. Administrative extents vary widely: for illustration, Paris proper is about 105 km², New York City about 783 km², Beijing municipality about 16,410 km², Shanghai municipality about 6,340 km², and Shenzhen about 1,997 km². Exact values depend on the boundary definition and treatment of water. Because the fixed 3 × 3 window is not clipped to administrative boundaries, it can extend far beyond a compact city such as Paris but cover only part of a geographically large municipality such as Beijing. It may therefore overrepresent surrounding areas for compact cities and underrepresent the full administrative extent of large municipalities. Results should be interpreted as city-centered metropolitan region estimates, not city-boundary totals. Deaths are summed across the window; PM2.5 is population-weighted over cells with valid PM2.5 support and population. Missing mortality cells are excluded without rescaling. Circles mark city coordinates only: color represents avoidable share and size represents avoidable deaths/year. Circles do not show city boundaries or window area, and nearby windows may overlap.
