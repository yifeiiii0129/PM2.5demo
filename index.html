Methods

Version 2 data and health model

Annual PM2.5 fields for 2020-2023 are taken from the version2 data folder and interpolated to the 0.25 degree GEMM grid. All-cause attributable deaths are estimated with GEMM using each year's GBD NCD+LRI baseline mortality, GPW 2020 population, and 2015 SSP1 age fractions. Avoidable deaths use the WHO 5 ug/m3 counterfactual.

Map Notes

Boundaries and city coverage

Country polygons, province/state boundaries, and aggregation masks use the World Bank official boundary files stored in version2/data. City points use SimpleMaps worldcities basic v1.91.1 populated places, and mortality values use the GEMM metro-area output for places above 500,000 people. This city layer is not an official administrative city list; full city matching outputs remain in version2/data/city data.
